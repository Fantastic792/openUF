## openUF 1.0.7

The full hostapd swap no longer leaves the radios down, WEP never comes up as an open
network, 2.4 GHz Auto stays on 1, 6 and 11, a radio whose Auto channel fails gets a channel,
and an AP cabled below another stops showing up as a wired client.

### What got fixed

- **The full hostapd swap could leave the radios down.** The swap (LuCI: openUF,
  Overview, or `setup.sh`) installs a full wpad build in place of the basic one, and could
  leave the wpad service disabled, so hostapd stayed stopped. openUF now enables the
  service and restarts it after the swap (proven on hardware).
- **A WEP WLAN came up as an open network.** So did an Enhanced Open WLAN pushed without
  its OWE setting. openUF cannot build either one, so it now skips them and writes a log
  line naming the WLAN (proven on hardware). Your other WLANs are not affected.
- **2.4 GHz on Auto could pick a channel such as 3 or 9** and overlap two neighbouring
  networks. It now picks only among 1, 6 and 11 (proven on hardware). A channel blacklist
  set in the controller narrows that further, and a channel list set by hand in OpenWrt
  is left alone (proven in tests, not on hardware).
- **A radio whose automatic channel selection failed stayed off the air**, with all its
  WLANs down and nothing trying again. When a radio on Auto has had no channel for a
  minute, openUF now gives it a channel that needs no radar check: 1, 6 or 11 on 2.4 GHz,
  36 or 149 on 5 GHz. It keeps that channel while the controller asks for Auto. Setting a
  fixed channel in the controller, or forgetting the AP, ends it (proven on hardware).
- **An AP cabled below another showed up as a wired client** of the AP above it, next to
  itself in the topology. A PC plugged into the lower AP was reported by both APs, so the
  controller kept moving it between them. Now a port where an LLDP neighbour says it is a
  bridge, router or access point reports no wired clients: that device reports its own
  (proven on hardware).

### Changed behaviour

- A 2.4 GHz radio on Auto with no channel blacklist used to be free to pick any channel.
  It now picks only 1, 6 or 11 (see above).
- A third-party managed switch below an AP's port hides its clients from the controller,
  since it announces itself as a bridge over LLDP.

### How to upgrade

In LuCI: System > Software > Update lists..., then the Updates tab: upgrade `openuf`, then
`luci-app-openuf`. Or over SSH: `apk update && apk upgrade`. The upgrade restarts openUF
on the new version.

### Known issues

- Upgrading `luci-app-openuf` in LuCI shows "Unable to execute apk upgrade command: XHR
  request aborted by browser". The upgrade worked: it restarts the web server while
  LuCI is still waiting for the answer. Reload the page.
- If openUF restarts while the LED is off, LED on leaves the other colours of the light
  off until the next reboot.
- Once openUF has given a radio a channel after a failed Auto selection, the radio stays on
  that channel even if Auto would work again later, until the controller sends a fixed
  channel or the AP is forgotten. It takes the first usable channel from the list, not the
  least busy one.
- The failed Auto selection was tested by taking the radio off the air by hand. A real
  failure of hostapd's channel selection was not reproduced on the JioRouter AX6000.
- The full lab test run was stopped part way to ship this release. Passed so far: the
  safety checks, and 925 of the 1034 controller settings, all put back cleanly. Not run
  yet on this release: the rest of the settings, the flash-write, install-cycle and
  bad-input checks, and the one-AP and two-AP tests (roaming, mesh). A problem they find
  will be fixed in 1.0.8.

### For the technically minded

- `openuf-deps` and `setup.sh`: `/etc/init.d/wpad enable` after the swap, before the
  restart.
- `inform.lua`: a WLAN with no `wpa` that is not enterprise or OWE transition, whose
  `security` is not `open`/`none` (WEP arrives as `security=wep64`) or that has
  `wpa3_support` with no OWE key management, is skipped.
- `ucihelper.rf_config`: on 2.4 GHz Auto with no controller channel list, writes
  `chanlist` = 1, 6 and 11 minus the site blacklist, stamped `openuf_chanlist`; a
  `chanlist` without the stamp is never touched.
- New `acsguard.lua` (a heartbeat tick): a radio on Auto with AP interfaces and no live
  channel in `iw dev` for 60 s gets `channel` pinned and stamped `openuf_acspin`;
  `rf_config` keeps the pin under Auto and drops it under a fixed channel; `forget()` puts
  Auto back.
- `inform.lua`: a port whose LLDP neighbour advertises the bridge, router or WLAN AP
  capability reports no wired hosts.
- Ideas for the first four from [antoniocosta/unifi-openwrt](https://github.com/antoniocosta/unifi-openwrt),
  written fresh.
- Full list of commits: compare `v1.0.6...v1.0.7` on GitHub.
