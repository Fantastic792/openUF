## openUF 1.0.6

Transmit Power Low and Medium work, LED off turns the whole light off, and Channel AI's
plans go on the air as planned if you want them to.

### What got fixed

- **Transmit Power Low and Medium ran at full power.** They now run below the radio's
  maximum: Medium 6 dB below, Low 12 dB below. On a radio whose maximum is 30 dBm that is
  24 dBm for Medium and 18 dBm for Low (proven on hardware). Ubiquiti publishes no table
  for these levels, so the steps are openUF's choice, not a copy of the stock firmware.
  Auto, High and a custom value in dBm are unchanged.
- **LED off left part of the light on.** On boards whose one light has several colours,
  openUF turned off only its own colour. On the JioRouter AX6000 (JIDU6401) the red part
  stayed lit. LED off now turns off every colour of that light, and LED on brings them
  back (proven on hardware).
- **Channel AI's Quick Scan swept every band on each radio** on boards where one radio
  can use several bands, and reported the other bands' channels as its own. Each radio now
  scans only its own band, which also makes the scan shorter (proven in the VM lab).

### New setting

- **Use the controller's channel width** (LuCI: openUF, Settings, WiFi behaviour; UCI
  `openuf.main.honour_controller_width`). Off by default, which keeps today's behaviour:
  some boards raise the controller's width to their own minimum (the JioRouter boards turn
  40 MHz into 80 MHz). On: the radios run exactly the width the controller sends, so a
  Channel AI plan goes on the air as planned (proven on hardware). If you turn it on and
  want 80 MHz, set it in the controller.

### How to upgrade

In LuCI: System > Software > Update lists..., then the Updates tab: upgrade `openuf`, then
`luci-app-openuf`. Or over SSH: `apk update && apk upgrade`. The upgrade restarts openUF
on the new version.

### Known issues

- Upgrading `luci-app-openuf` in LuCI shows "Unable to execute apk upgrade command: XHR
  request aborted by browser". The upgrade worked: it restarts the web server while
  LuCI is still waiting for the answer. Reload the page.
- If openUF restarts while the LED is off, LED on leaves the other colours of the light
  off until the next reboot.

### For the technically minded

- `inform.lua`: `radio.<n>.txpower_mode=low|medium` (sent with `txpower=auto`) now reaches
  `rf_config`, which writes `txpower = max - 12|6` from the band's driver maximum
  (`iw phy`), or leaves the driver's maximum when that is unknown.
- `led.lua`: `set_enabled` also drives the sibling LEDs (same vendor prefix and function,
  e.g. `red:status` for `green:status`), remembering their trigger and brightness.
- `sysinfo.phy_frequencies` keeps only the band of the interface's current frequency.
- `ucihelper.rf_config`: the board's `htmode_floor` is skipped when
  `honour_controller_width` is on.
- Full list of commits: compare `v1.0.5...v1.0.6` on GitHub.
